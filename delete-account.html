Delete Your GanitKaksha Account

You can request deletion of your GanitKaksha account and the personal data linked to it in either of these ways.

Option 1: Inside the app

  1. Log in to the GanitKaksha app.
  2. Go to the Delete Account option.
  3. Confirm your request.

Option 2: By email

Send an email to ganitkaksha@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Please mention your full name and registered email so we can find your account.

What gets deleted

  • Full name, email address, phone number
  • School name, year and gender
  • Password and account activation details

Timeline

  • We process your request within 7 days.
  • There is a 30-day grace period after the request. If you log in during this time, your account is restored.
  • If you do not log in within 30 days, your account and data are permanently deleted.
