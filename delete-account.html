Delete Your Ganit Kaksha Account

Disclaimer: Ganit Kaksha is an independent educational app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing WBBSE or any government body. Official source: https://wbbse.wb.gov.in

You can delete your Ganit Kaksha account and all the personal data linked to it in either of these ways.

Option 1: Inside the app (instant)

  1. Log in to the Ganit Kaksha app.
  2. Open the menu and tap Delete My Account.
  3. Type CONFIRM and tap the delete button.

Your account and data are deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.

Option 2: By email

If you cannot log in, send an email to ganitkaksha@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Please mention your full name and registered email so we can find your account. We process email requests within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Full name, email address, phone number
  • School name, year and gender
  • Quiz attempts, scores and statistics
  • Password and account activation details

Retention

Server backups and logs, if any, may keep data for up to 30 days, after which they are removed.
