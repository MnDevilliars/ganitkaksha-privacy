1. Introduction

Ganit Kaksha ("the App", "we", "our") is a learning app for school students, developed and operated by Md Noorain Ansari, an individual developer based in India. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have. By registering and using the App, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

When you register for an account, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • School name
  • Year selected during registration
  • Gender
  • Password (stored in protected form)

While you use the App, we also keep:

  • Your quiz attempts, scores, progress and performance statistics
  • Whether your account is free or activated (Pro), and the activation key linked to your account
  • Technical data such as IP address and basic server logs, used for security and to prevent misuse

If you send us a message through the contact form, your name, email and message are sent by email to our support inbox. They are not stored in our database.

If you contact us outside the App (for example on WhatsApp), we can see your number and the messages you send.

3. Why we collect this information

  • To create and manage your account and keep it secure
  • To show your progress, weak topics and leaderboard rank
  • To activate Pro features on your account
  • To send account-related emails and answer your questions
  • To improve the App and prevent misuse

4. Leaderboard

The App has a leaderboard. Other users can see your name and your rank, which is based on your score. They cannot see your other personal information.

5. AI answer checking

For Pro users, answers typed for short-answer questions are sent to Google Gemini so they can be checked. Please do not enter personal information in your answers.

6. Where your data is stored and who processes it

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. To run the App, we use these service providers: MongoDB Atlas (database), Render (hosting), Cloudflare R2 (file storage), Resend and Brevo (emails), and Google Gemini (AI answer checking). Your data may also be disclosed if required by law. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for students aged 13 and above. It is not intended for children under 13. Students under 18 should register and use the App only with the knowledge and permission of a parent or guardian. We collect only the information needed to run the App. A parent or guardian may contact us at any time to review or delete their child's data.

8. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account in either of these ways:

  • Use the Delete My Account option inside the App. Your account and all data linked to it are deleted immediately after you confirm.
  • Email us at ganitkaksha@gmail.com from your registered email address. We process email requests within 7 days.

More details are available on our Account Deletion page.

9. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. After you delete your account, your personal data is removed from our database. Server backups and logs, if any, may keep data for a short period (up to 30 days) before they are removed.

10. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by emailing us at the address below.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Please keep your password and activation key confidential.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
