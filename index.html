Privacy Policy

App: GanitKaksha
Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. Introduction

GanitKaksha ("the App", "we", "our") is a learning app for school students, developed and operated by Md Noorain Ansari, an individual developer based in India. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have. By registering and using the App, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

When you register for an account, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • School name
  • Year selected during registration
  • Gender
  • Password (stored in protected form)

We also keep basic account information such as whether your account is free or activated (Pro), and the activation key linked to your account.

If you contact us on WhatsApp to get an activation key, we can see the WhatsApp number and the messages you send us. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that service.

3. Why we collect this information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To let you log in and keep your account secure
  • To verify your identity when you ask for an activation key
  • To activate Pro features on your account
  • To contact you about your account or to answer your questions
  • To improve the App and provide support

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in a MongoDB database. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for advertising. Your data may be accessed only by us and by the service providers needed to run the App (for example, our database and hosting provider), or if required by law.

6. Children's privacy

The App is intended for school students, some of whom may be under 18 years of age. Students under 18 should use the App and register only with the knowledge and permission of a parent or guardian. We collect only the information needed to run the App. A parent or guardian may contact us at any time to review or delete their child's data.

7. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and data in either of these ways:

  • Use the Delete Account option inside the App, or
  • Email us at ganitkaksha@gmail.com from your registered email address.

We will process your deletion request within 7 days. After a deletion request, there is a 30-day grace period: if you log in during these 30 days, your account is restored. If you do not log in within 30 days, your account and personal data are permanently deleted.

More details are available on our Account Deletion page.

8. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. After a deletion request, data is retained only for the 30-day grace period described above, and then permanently deleted.

9. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by emailing us at the address below.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Please keep your password and activation key confidential.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add new features such as notifications or payments. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
